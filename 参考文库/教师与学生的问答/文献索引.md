# 教师与学生的问答 — 文献索引

## 一、基础理论

### 1. Bloom's 2 Sigma Problem (1984)
- **Bloom, B. S. (1984).** The 2 Sigma Problem: The Search for Methods of Group Instruction as Effective as One-to-One Tutoring. *Educational Researcher, 13*(6), 4–16.
- 核心发现：一对一答疑效果是课堂授课的 2 倍标准差（2 sigma），tutored student 超过 98% 的控制组学生
- 关键变量：纠正性反馈、针对漏洞的精准解释、学生主动参与、动机强化、前序知识不跳过

### 2. Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)
- **Vygotsky, L. S. (1978).** *Mind in Society: The Development of Higher Psychological Processes.* Harvard University Press.
- **Wood, D., Bruner, J. S., & Ross, G. (1976).** The Role of Tutoring in Problem Solving. *Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 17*(2), 89–100.
- 核心概念：学生能独立完成的（Zone of Actual Development）vs 需要帮助才能完成的（ZPD），教师应该在 ZPD 内提供"刚好够"的帮助（scaffolding）

### 3. Cognitive Apprenticeship (1989)
- **Collins, A., Brown, J. S., & Newman, S. E. (1989).** Cognitive Apprenticeship: Teaching the Crafts of Reading, Writing, and Mathematics. In L. B. Resnick (Ed.), *Knowing, Learning, and Instruction* (pp. 453–494).
- 四维框架：Content（领域知识+策略）、Methods（Modeling→Coaching→Scaffolding→Fading）、Sequence（复杂度递增）、Sociology（真实情境）
- **Brown, J. S., Collins, A., & Duguid, P. (1989).** Situated Cognition and the Culture of Learning. *Educational Researcher, 18*(1), 32–42.

### 4. Professional Vision & Teacher Noticing
- **Goodwin, C. (1994).** Professional Vision. *American Anthropologist, 96*(3), 606–633.
- **Sherin, M. G. (2007).** The Development of Teachers' Professional Vision in Video Clubs. In R. Goldman et al. (Eds.), *Video Research in the Learning Sciences.*
- 核心概念：专家教师"看到的"和新手不同——coding schemes（编码框架）、highlighting（标记关键信息）、material representations（外部表征）

## 二、专家教师决策模型

### 5. Effective Tutoring Techniques (1992)
- **Merrill, D. C., Reiser, B. J., Ranney, M., & Trafton, J. G. (1992).** Effective Tutoring Techniques: A Comparison of Human Tutors and Intelligent Tutoring Systems. *Journal of the Learning Sciences, 2*(3), 277–305. (305+ citations)
- 核心发现：人类教师比 ITS 更灵活、更微妙；两者都聚焦于从"卡住"的状态恢复；人类教师使用更多样化的支架策略

### 6. Motivational Techniques of Expert Tutors (1993)
- **Lepper, M. R., Woolverton, M., Mumme, D. L., & Gurtner, J. L. (1993).** Motivational Techniques of Expert Human Tutors: Lessons for the Design of Computer-Based Tutors. In S. P. Lajoie & S. J. Derry (Eds.), *Computers as Cognitive Tools.*
- 核心发现：专家教师同时平衡认知支持和动机支持；支架逐步撤销（fading）

### 7. BRIDGE: 新手-专家差距 (2024, NAACL)
- **Wang, R. E. et al. (2024).** Bridging the Novice-Expert Gap via Models of Decision-Making: A Case Study on Remediating Math Mistakes. *NAACL 2024.*
- GitHub: https://github.com/rosewang2008/bridge
- 核心发现：
  - 认知任务分析法拆解 700 段真实数学辅导对话
  - 专家三步决策：错误分类(A) → 策略选择(B) → 意图设定(C)
  - GPT-4 注入专家决策后评分 +76%；随机决策 −97%
  - LLM 决策路径单一（总是直接解释），人类专家路径多样

## 三、诊断与对话模式

### 8. Diagnostic Models for Procedural Bugs (1978)
- **Brown, J. S., & Burton, R. R. (1978).** Diagnostic Models for Procedural Bugs in Basic Mathematical Skills. *Cognitive Science, 2*(2), 155–192.
- 核心发现：数学错误不是随机的，而是系统性"bug"——专家诊断应识别错误模式

### 9. Collaborative Dialogue Patterns (1995)
- **Graesser, A. C., Person, N. K., & Magliano, J. P. (1995).** Collaborative Dialogue Patterns in Naturalistic One-to-One Tutoring. *Applied Cognitive Psychology, 9*(6), 495–522.
- 五步对话框架：提问 → 学生回答 → 简短反馈 → 追问解释 → 提供支架

### 10. Teacher Diagnostic Reasoning (2024)
- **Rieu, A., Loibl, K., & Leuders, T. (2024).** How Context Influences the Processing of Relevant Information and Judgment Accuracy in Diagnosing Misconceptions. *Frontiers in Psychology, 15*, 1405756.
- **Kosel, C., Bauer, E., & Seidel, T. (2024).** Where Experience Makes a Difference: Teachers' Judgment Accuracy and Diagnostic Reasoning. *Frontiers in Psychology, 15*, 1278472.
- 核心发现：限制信息量反而提高诊断准确性（强制深度加工）；经验丰富的教师连接更丰富的表层+深层线索

### 11. SocraticLM (2024, NeurIPS)
- **Liu, Chen, Huang et al. (2024).** SocraticLM: Exploring Socratic Personalized Teaching with Large Language Models. *NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight.*
- 核心发现：首个大规模实现苏格拉底教学法的 LLM；多智能体 pipeline（Dean-Teacher-Student）；在 5 个教学质量维度超越 GPT-4

### 12. Math Recovery (2013)
- **Smith, T. M., Cobb, P., Farran, D. C., Cordray, D. S., & Munter, C. (2013).** Evaluating Math Recovery: Assessing the Causal Impact of a Diagnostic Tutoring Program on Student Achievement. *American Educational Research Journal, 50*(2), 397–428.
- 核心发现：诊断性一对一辅导需要持续的contingent assessment——教师不断重新诊断、不断调整

## 四、新增文献（2026-05-14）

### 13. ChatBack — 同步纠错聊天机器人 (2023)
- **Liang, K.-H. et al. (2023).** ChatBack: Investigating Strategies of Providing Synchronous Grammatical Error Feedback in a GUI-based Language Learning Social Chatbot. *Proceedings of the 18th Workshop on BEA (ACL 2023)*, 83–99.
- 核心发现：(1) 反馈放在独立 GUI 比在聊天里好；(2) 引导自我纠错优于直接给答案；(3) 策略效果因学生水平和动机而异

### 14. GenAI 在教学实践中的系统综述 (2025)
- **Wang Xiaoyu, Zainuddin, Z. & Chin Hai Leng (2025).** Generative Artificial Intelligence in Pedagogical Practices: A Systematic Review of Empirical Studies (2022–2024). *Cogent Education, 12*(1), 2485499.
- 核心发现：28 篇 SSCI 综述，四大效益（效率/参与/创意/技能），三大挑战（偏见/诚信/效果），建议 AI 作补充工具

### 15. AI 学习工具对学术创新的影响 (2025)
- **Younas, M., El-Dakhs, D.A.S. & Noor, U. (2025).** The Impact of Artificial Intelligence-Based Learning Tools in Academic Innovation. *Frontiers in Education, 10*, 1689205.
- 核心发现：ChatGPT/DeepSeek/Gemini 综述，需算法透明度 + 人机协同

### 16. 数字教育研究前沿 (2025)
- **Tran Ai Cam & Nguyen Huu Thanh Chung (2025).** Impactful Research Fronts in Digital Educational Ecosystem. *Frontiers in Education*.
- 核心发现：AI 和在线学习是最大研究前沿，发展中国家贡献在增长

### 17. AI 在教师教育中的应用 (2024)
- **Meylani, R. (2024).** Artificial Intelligence in the Education of Teachers: A Qualitative Synthesis. *Journal of Computer and Education Research, 12*(24), 600–637.
- 核心发现：ITS/VR/AI分析改善教师教育，但教师 AI 素养缺口大，需伦理指南
